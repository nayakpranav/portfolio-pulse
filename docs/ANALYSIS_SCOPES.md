# Analysis scopes and availability

Personal analysis defaults to **Stocks & funds** when current derivative
quotations are missing. The scope selector changes presentation only. The entire
uploaded export, stock/fund FIFO, derivative ledger, settlements and historical
accounting remain in the unchanged V6.7.8 pipeline.

| Figure | Required evidence | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stocks & funds value | Valid stock accounting and every active stock/fund valued | Stocks & funds; cash and derivatives excluded |
| Stocks & funds lifetime profit | Canonical open FIFO P/L + realized FIFO P/L + net dividends; reconciled against both the stock/fund cash-flow ledger and contribution rows | Stocks & funds; cash interest, derivative results and promotional personal benefit excluded |
| Tracked investment value / lifetime economic profit | Complete ecosystem accounting and every material required current valuation | Full portfolio; original canonical definitions retained |
| Net user capital committed / recovery | Valid complete transaction and recognized-income ledgers | Full investment ecosystem, including derivative transactions; current quotes not required |
| Stock/fund MWR | Valid stock accounting, canonical dated flows and terminal value; unique valid root | Stocks & funds; annualized |
| Stock/fund TWR / periods / wealth chart | Valid stock accounting and canonical historical coverage | Stocks & funds; cumulative returns |
| Benchmark MWR / matched wealth | Exact selected benchmark, suitable adjusted history, reliable EUR conversion and matched dates | Stock/fund cash-flow-matched PME; annualized MWR |
| YTD investment income | Independently reconciled net dividend and interest ledgers, with actual source cutoff | Recognized income; reinvested dividends recognized once |

A missing stock/fund quote blocks its current subtotal and stock/fund profit;
reliable historical TWR and income can remain available. The holdings table lists
unpriced positions explicitly; ranking weights cover valued stock/fund assets.
Unpriced derivatives do not make the stock/fund ranking partial.

Known low-confidence historical fallbacks retain the canonical status and a
visible warning. Incomplete historical coverage suppresses return charts/periods,
without hiding valid current holdings. Failed benchmarks remain unavailable;
there is no substitution. Duplicate identifiers, unmatched stock sales,
unsupported corporate actions and unmatched private events remain fail-closed.
A proven derivative-only basis blocker leaves stock/fund accounting independent
but blocks full-ecosystem capital and recovery.

The report exposes accounting, current valuation and historical causes separately.
It prioritizes specific accounting causes ahead of aggregated unknown-row counts
or derivative warnings. An unclassified accounting failure stays blocked.
The PDF identifies the selected scope and explicitly displays full-portfolio
availability even when the headline value/profit cards use Stocks & funds.

Private established event evidence loads automatically from the Windows personal
profile, including personal launches without an explicit directory override.
It is never distributed. Canonical identity, quantity, zero-consideration and
supported-prior-position safeguards still apply.
